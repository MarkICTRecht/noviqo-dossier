**BACKGROUND**
(A) Dendra provides access to its proprietary large language models through an application programming interface (the "Dendra Platform").
(B) Customer operates the software-as-a-service platform PlanGrip and intends to use the Dendra Platform to generate natural-language summaries within its forthcoming module PlanGrip Insights.
(C) This Agreement consists of these Enterprise Terms, the Order Form (Exhibit A), the Data Processing Addendum including the Standard Contractual Clauses (Exhibit B), and the Dendra Service Level Policy and Acceptable Use Policy (version 2025.3) as published at the Effective Date, which are incorporated by reference. In the event of conflict, Exhibit B prevails over Exhibit A, which prevails over these Enterprise Terms.

**NOW, THEREFORE, the Parties agree as follows:**

## Section 1 – Definitions
1.1 "Customer Content" means all Inputs and Outputs.
1.2 "Input" means any prompt, text, file or other data submitted to the Dendra Platform by or on behalf of Customer, including through Customer's end users.
1.3 "Output" means any content generated by the Dendra Platform in response to an Input.
1.4 "Zero Data Retention" or "ZDR" means the configuration described in Section 5.4 under which Inputs and Outputs are processed in memory only and are not written to persistent storage by Dendra, except as strictly necessary to return the Output.

## Section 2 – Access and Use
2.1 Subject to Customer's compliance with this Agreement and payment of the Fees, Dendra grants Customer a non-exclusive, non-transferable, non-sublicensable (except to Authorized Users as part of Customer's products) right during the Term to access and use the Dendra Platform via the API keys issued to Customer, solely for the use case described in the Order Form.
2.2 Customer shall not (a) use the Dendra Platform to develop models that compete with Dendra; (b) reverse engineer, decompile or attempt to extract model weights; (c) circumvent rate limits or safety features; (d) use the Dendra Platform for any purpose prohibited by the Acceptable Use Policy; or (e) represent that Outputs are human-generated where applicable law requires disclosure.
2.3 Dendra may suspend access without liability if Customer's use poses a security risk, materially breaches the Acceptable Use Policy, or if an undisputed invoice is more than thirty (30) days overdue, with prior notice where reasonably practicable.

## Section 3 – Fees and Payment
3.1 Customer shall pay the fees set out in the Order Form (the "Fees"). All Fees are stated and payable in United States dollars and are exclusive of taxes. Customer shall account for any value added tax under the reverse-charge mechanism where applicable.
3.2 The committed annual spend is non-cancellable and non-refundable, except as expressly set out in Section 11.3. Usage exceeding the committed spend is invoiced monthly in arrears at the overage rates in the Order Form.
3.3 Invoices are due thirty (30) days from the invoice date. Late payments bear interest at the lesser of 1.0% per month or the maximum rate permitted by law.

## Section 4 – Ownership of Content
4.1 As between the Parties, Customer retains all right, title and interest in its Inputs and, to the extent permitted by applicable law, owns all Outputs. Dendra hereby assigns to Customer any right it may have in the Outputs.
4.2 Outputs may not be unique, and Dendra makes no representation that Outputs are protected by any intellectual property right.
4.3 Dendra retains all right, title and interest in the Dendra Platform, the models, the Documentation and all improvements thereto.
4.4 **No training.** Dendra shall not use Customer Content to train, retrain or fine-tune any model, nor otherwise to improve the Dendra Platform, unless Customer has given its express prior written opt-in. No such opt-in has been given as of the Effective Date.

## Section 5 – Data Protection and Security
5.1 To the extent Dendra processes personal data on behalf of Customer in providing the Dendra Platform, the Data Processing Addendum attached as Exhibit B applies and forms an integral part of this Agreement.
5.2 Dendra maintains a written information security program no less protective than Annex II to Exhibit B, as well as a current SOC 2 Type II report and ISO/IEC 27001 certification.
5.3 **Standard retention.** Unless ZDR applies, Dendra retains Inputs and Outputs for up to thirty (30) days solely for the purpose of abuse and misuse monitoring and for troubleshooting at Customer's request, after which they are deleted from active systems. Such retained data is accessible only to a limited number of authorized Dendra trust-and-safety personnel.
5.4 **Zero Data Retention option.** Customer may request that ZDR be enabled for one or more of its API projects. Enabling ZDR is subject to Dendra's written approval, which Dendra will not unreasonably withhold for customers on an Enterprise plan, and may require Customer to implement its own abuse monitoring in accordance with the Documentation. Upon enablement, Section 5.3 shall cease to apply to the relevant API projects. As of the Effective Date, ZDR is available to Customer but has not been enabled (see Order Form).
5.5 Customer shall not submit to the Dendra Platform (a) special categories of personal data within the meaning of Article 9 of the GDPR, (b) government identification numbers, or (c) payment card data, except as permitted in the Order Form. Customer shall implement pseudonymization of personal data prior to submission where feasible for the use case.

## Exhibit B – Data Processing Addendum (summary of key provisions)
B.1 **Roles.** Customer acts as processor on behalf of its own customers (the controllers) and, for limited account and support data, as controller. Dendra acts as Customer's (sub-)processor and processes personal data only on Customer's documented instructions, which consist of this Agreement and Customer's API calls.
B.2 **Data.** Inputs may contain pseudonymized planning data relating to employees of Customer's customers (pseudonymous identifiers, job role, team, project allocation, availability, aggregated absence indicators). Names and contact details are replaced by pseudonyms before submission (Section 5.5).
B.3 **Security and confidentiality.** Dendra binds its personnel to confidentiality and applies the measures in Annex II, including TLS 1.2 or higher, AES-256 encryption at rest, role-based access controls and annual third-party penetration testing.
B.4 **Sub-processors.** Customer grants general authorization for the sub-processors listed on Dendra's sub-processor page at the Effective Date, including the cloud infrastructure providers in the United States and Ireland. Dendra gives fifteen (15) days' notice of new sub-processors by email. If an objection cannot be resolved, Customer may terminate the affected services as its sole remedy, with a pro-rata refund.
B.5 **Breach notification.** Dendra notifies Customer of a personal data breach without undue delay and in any event within forty-eight (48) hours after becoming aware.
B.6 **Audits.** Dendra makes available its SOC 2 Type II report and ISO/IEC 27001 certificate. Where these are demonstrably insufficient, Customer may audit once per year upon thirty (30) days' notice, at Customer's cost.
B.7 **Deletion.** Upon termination, Dendra deletes all personal data within thirty (30) days, unless retention is required by law.
B.8 **Transfers.** Dendra represents that it is self-certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and will notify Customer promptly if it ceases to be certified. Transfers to the United States rely on the European Commission's adequacy decision of July 10, 2023.
B.9 **Standard Contractual Clauses.** As a fallback that applies automatically if the adequacy decision is invalidated or Dendra's certification lapses, the Parties enter into the Standard Contractual Clauses of Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/914, Module Three (processor to processor) and, where Customer acts as controller, Module Two, incorporated by reference. Clause 7 (docking) applies; Clause 9(a) Option 2 applies with the notice period in B.4; Clauses 17 and 18: law and courts of Ireland. Annexes I to III are completed in accordance with this Exhibit and the Order Form.
